==============================================================
SITE: PetPalace — All Creatures Great & Small
==============================================================

ROLE / CONTEXT
--------------
Role: Lead Designer & Brand Strategist for a premium pet supply and care brand.
Context: We are launching "PetPalace," an online emporium that caters to ALL domestic pets,
not just cats and dogs. Our unique selling point is "Class-Specific Curation" —
every type of animal gets its own distinct visual identity, tailored accessories,
and care guides. The website must feel like walking into a beautiful, modern boutique
where every section is thoughtfully designed for its specific resident.

PURPOSE
-------
An e-commerce and informational hub for pet owners. Users can shop accessories,
book grooming/health consultations, and read care guides. The site must visually
and structurally differentiate between animal classes:
1. Dogs (Canines)
2. Cats (Felines)
3. Fish (Aquatics)
4. Reptiles (Herpetology)
5. Amphibians (Herpetology - distinct visual split)
6. Birds (Avians)


==============================================================
STEP 2 — BRAND VOICE (for `brand` skill)
==============================================================
Voice archetype: The Informed Curator — Knowledgeable, warm, and inclusive.
Speaks with authority on pet care but never talks down. Celebrates the quirks
of every creature.

Tone dimensions:
- Formal ↔ Casual: 3 (Conversational Professional)
- Serious ↔ Playful: 4 (Warm and inviting)
- Reserved ↔ Expressive: 4 (Enthusiastic about pets)

Voice rules:
- NEVER refer to pets as "it." Use "they" or specific species.
- Mention "habitats" instead of "cages" or "tanks" for non-mammals.
- For dogs: energetic, loyal, "pack walks." For cats: curious, serene, "window perches."

Tagline: "Curated Care for Every Kingdom."
Key Phrases: "Beyond Cats & Dogs," "Find Your Flock," "Scale & Tail Care."


==============================================================
STEP 3 — DESIGN SYSTEM (for `ui-ux-pro-max` + `design-system`)
==============================================================
Style keywords: Modern Boutique, Soft Minimalism, Organized, Color-Coded, Airy.
Preferred style direction: Clean, card-based layout. White space is crucial.
Subtle use of soft shadows (Soft Claymorphism or Neumorphism light).

GLOBAL COLOR SYSTEM (Base):
- Surface-base: Clean White (#FFFFFF) or Soft Cream (#F9F9F9)
- Surface-raised: Pure White (#FFFFFF) with light border
- Ink: Dark Charcoal (#2C2C2C) for readability
- Global Accent: Warm Sand or Greige (used for neutral buttons, footer)

CLASS-SPECIFIC COLOR PALETTES (Used for Hero banners, category cards, and badges):
- Dogs (Canines): Warm Terracotta & Deep Navy. (Reliable, strong, energetic).
- Cats (Felines): Soft Lavender & Sage Green. (Serene, playful, regal).
- Fish (Aquatics): Deep Teal & Coral Pop. (Fluid, vibrant, underwater).
- Reptiles: Sunbaked Ochre & Terracotta. (Arid, ancient, textural).
- Amphibians: Fresh Lime Green & Pond Brown. (Vivacious, damp, bright).
- Birds: Sky Blue & Soft Sunshine Yellow. (Airy, light, chirpy).

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Display: "Playfair Display" (Elegant, premium feel for headers).
- Body: "Nunito Sans" or "Inter" (Highly readable, friendly, modern).
- Type scale: 1.200 (Major Second).

SPACING & RADII:
- Container max-width: 1400px (to allow wider product grids).
- Card Radius: 24px (lg).
- Button Radius: Pill (9999px).

==============================================================
STEP 5 — SECTIONS & LAYOUT (for Stitch)
==============================================================
Section order and goal:

1. Hero — Full-width dynamic banner. Should rotate or feature the 6 animal classes subtly.
   Headline: "Curated Care for Every Kingdom." CTA: "Shop by Pet Type".
2. Category Hub (The 6 Kingdoms) — 6 interactive cards. Each card uses its specific
   Class Color Palette. Card shows Icon + Name (Dogs, Cats, Fish, Reptiles, Amphibians, Birds).
3. Featured Products — Carousel of best-sellers. Badges on products must match the
   Class Color of the animal they belong to.
4. The "Pet Care" Service Menu — 3 cards (Grooming, Vet Telehealth, Nutrition Consult).
5. Adoption Spotlight — A dedicated section for rescue animals (placeholder images).
6. Blog / Care Guides — 3 cards with tips for different classes.
7. Testimonials — Quotes from happy owners of all pet types.
8. Footer — Standard links, newsletter signup.
